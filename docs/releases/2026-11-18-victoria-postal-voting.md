# 18 November 2026 Victoria election voting information

VEC postal vote applications close at 6 pm AEDT on Wednesday 25 November. The VEC advises voters in Victoria to post or drop off a completed ballot before 6 pm AEDT on Saturday 28 November. The media handbook and scrutineer handbook confirm the receipt cutoff of 6 pm AEDT on Friday 4 December. The official how-to-vote card register and counting timetable are linked separately.
