# Esperance Town Ward extraordinary election nominations

Esperance Town Ward: the Shire lists four nominees for one vacancy. Official nomination profiles give the ballot-paper names Wayne Halliday, Michael Hart, David Kinyai Mureithi and Dale Piercey. Names are listed here by surname. Ballot order has not been verified.

Postal ballots must reach the returning officer by 6 pm AWST on Thursday 3 December 2026.
