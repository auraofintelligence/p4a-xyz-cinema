# Gingin councillor vacancy agenda, 14 November 2026

Jason Weeks, elected in October 2023, resigned as councillor effective 3 November 2026. The 17 November meeting agenda (pages 7–9) says the Electoral Commissioner approved allowing the vacancy to remain until the 2027 ordinary election. Officers recommend that Council note the approval and resolve to leave the position unfilled until that election. The Council decision and any replacement timetable await confirmation.
