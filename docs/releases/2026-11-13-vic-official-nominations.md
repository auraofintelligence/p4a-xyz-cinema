# 2026 Victorian state election · official nomination totals and voting timetable

The Victorian Electoral Commission published 1,037 nominations on 10 November: 734 for the Legislative Assembly and 303 for the Legislative Council. Nominations closed at noon AEDT on 9 November. The VEC directory gives the ballot order for every district and region. The 495 P4A announcement and preselection records remain dated source observations. Early voting in Victoria runs 18–27 November, 9 am–6 pm, with centres closed on Sunday 22 November. Election day voting is 28 November, 8 am–6 pm AEDT.
