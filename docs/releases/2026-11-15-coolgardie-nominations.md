# Coolgardie extraordinary election nominations

Coolgardie’s Shire publishes three candidates for one district councillor position with a term ending 16 October 2027. Nominations closed 20 October at 4 pm AWST. Completed postal voting packages must arrive by 6 pm AWST on 3 December. Checked 15 November.

The official profile artwork confirms FREITAG, Carolyn, LEESON, Wade, and WILLIS, Renee. The fourth image supplements Freitag’s profile. The public list is arranged alphabetically by surname. The published ballot order and affiliations remain unverified.
