# 14 November 2026 · King Island Council election

Andrea Bowden and Saverina Stella won the two contested councillor seats after five councillors were elected unopposed. Sarina Laidler won the mayoral ballot and Ian Allan won the deputy mayoral ballot.

King Island Council’s 10 November notice gives six declarations of office, including Sarina Laidler as Mayor and Ian Allan as Deputy Mayor. David Bowden was elected, and the notice says he had yet to make his declaration. The prior TEC roster is archived.
