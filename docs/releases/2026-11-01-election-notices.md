3. **Tasmanian council counting:** the [TEC update displayed on its Hobart results page](https://www.tec.tas.gov.au/local-government/elections-2026/results/hobart-city/index.html), timestamped 31 October at 10:30 pm AEDT, says the last two deputy-mayor elections completed that day. Councillor counting adjourned and resumes 2 November; some councillor results are already final. This does not establish statewide completion, individual winners or changes of office.
4. **Milngin Ward:** the [live Victoria Daly Council article](https://www.victoriadaly.nt.gov.au/could-i-be-a-councillor/) now directly confirms a **3 December 2026** replacement election. Nominations run 9 November to 20 November noon; early/mobile voting starts 23 November; early voting closes 2 December at 6 pm; polling is 3 December, 8 am–6 pm; declaration is scheduled for 17 December. Times are Australia/Darwin, ACST (UTC+09:30). The failed August zero-nomination round remains separate. No candidates or effective vacancy day are inferred.
