# 28 November 2026 Walcha Council detailed by-election timetable

NSWEC's event page, checked on 28 November, now supplies the detailed timetable for its one-councillor by-election on 16 January 2027. The page displays an update label of 26 November 2026 at 3:49 PM; this is not asserted to be every row's first-publication date.

NSWEC timetable checked 28 November 2026: Postal vote applications open 30 November. Nominations open 7 December and close 16 December at noon; no opening hour is printed. Residential rolls and Braille applications close 7 December at 6 pm. The ballot draw is 17 December at 10 am, and postal packs begin distribution 21 December. Electoral material registration begins 16 December and closes 8 January at 5 pm. Candidate campaign registrations close 16 December; third-party campaigner registrations close 11 January, with no hours printed. Early voting opens 9 January and closes 15 January at 6 pm. Telephone-assisted applications and voting open 11 January at 8:30 am for eligible electors who are blind or have low vision; applications close 15 January at 6 pm and telephone voting closes 16 January at 1 pm. Postal applications close 11 January at 5 pm. Election-day voting is 16 January, 8 am to 6 pm. Postal vote receipt closes 29 January at 6 pm. Count completion on 1 February and declaration on 2 February are scheduled activities, not completed outcomes. Times are reproduced as published; the NSWEC timetable does not print a timezone label. The election-day counter marks the start of 16 January in Sydney. Polling hours are listed separately.
