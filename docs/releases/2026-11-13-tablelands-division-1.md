# Tablelands Division 1 by-election, 13 November 2026

The [Electoral Commission of Queensland's declared result](https://results.elections.qld.gov.au/TRCDiv126/01/primary) records Gregory Kevin English as elected. Its published first-preference count totals 2,345 formal votes across four candidates. [Tablelands Regional Council](https://www.trc.qld.gov.au/new-division-one-councillor-declared/) confirms that Greg English made his declaration of office and took up the role at 3 pm AEST on 11 November.

The [review](../../content/places/sources/tablelands-division-1-review-2026-11-13.json) preserves the earlier event snapshot and the separate postal deadlines. This update covers the Division 1 result and current office, the Queensland election summary and affected map and directory entries.
