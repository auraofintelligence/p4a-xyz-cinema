# 14 November 2026 · Tasman Council election

Alan John Hull won the contested councillor seat after six councillors were elected unopposed. Rod MacDonald won the mayoral ballot and Steve McQueeney won the deputy mayoral ballot.

The Council’s published directory still shows its pre-election membership, including Maria Stacey as Deputy Mayor. The July 2026 TEC roster is archived. The 2026 election results below give the elected names; current declarations of office remain unverified.
