# 28 November 2026 - Milngin early-voting hours

NTEC's voting-options page and dated 20 November media release give Darwin early voting from 30 November to 2 December, 8:30 am to 4 pm. The Victoria Daly council and Milngin Ward current-event records correct the 2 December early-voting close from 6 pm to 4 pm. The NT summary reflects this scoped correction.

The summary timetable updated 23 November instead prints 23 November at 8:30 am for early voting. Both detailed HTML sources and the linked detailed programme give 30 November at 8:30 am; that opening is retained with the conflict explicit. Mobile voting remains separately scheduled from 23 November at 9 am.

The two declared candidate records and their 21 November check dates remain unchanged, including the MCTAGGART/McTahhart spelling discrepancy. The event date, polling and postal deadline instants, scheduled result declaration, earlier failed round, historical ballots, current-office records and geometry are preserved. Application, dispatch, ballot completion, receipt and counting remain separate milestones. The existing Australia/Darwin timezone basis is retained; no newly printed ACST wording is claimed.

The official web copies were readable on 28 November, with individual crawl ages recorded in the source review. Ordinary native NTEC requests returned HTTP 403. PDF text corroborates the hours, but its screenshot returned cache miss; the changed closing hour is independently supported by both HTML sources.
