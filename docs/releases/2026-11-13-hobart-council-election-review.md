# 13 November 2026 · Hobart City Council

The City of Hobart lists Charles Wooley as Lord Mayor and eight other councillors. The TEC’s final results cover 32 councillor, 11 Lord Mayor and 10 Deputy Lord Mayor candidacies. Wooley won the Lord Mayor ballot with 55.04% in the final count. Ben Lohberger won the deputy ballot with 56.87%. He did not win a councillor seat and could not take up the office. The elected councillors will select the Deputy Lord Mayor.

The ordinary postal poll closed at 2 pm AEDT on 27 October. The TEC records a 3 November certificate issue date. The review covers the current roster and three final ballots.
