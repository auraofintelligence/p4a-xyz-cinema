# South Australian periodic council election timetable

Added the published 2027 SA periodic council timetable to 66 already confirmed ordinary events. Rolls close on 4 January at 5 pm. Nominations run from 26 January to 9 February at noon. Ballot packs are mailed from 11 to 17 March, and voting closes on 7 April at 5 pm. The newly recorded timetable entries retain null timezones.

The official timetable was checked on 20 November 2026. It does not publish an opening hour for nominations or name timezones. Existing postal-close timestamps retain their prior evidence. Coober Pedy and all other records outside the confirmed 7 April ordinary-event set retain their separate arrangements. Individual candidate, member and broad source-check dates retain their existing meanings.
