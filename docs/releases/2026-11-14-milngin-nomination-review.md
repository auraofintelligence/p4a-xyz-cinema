# 14 November 2026 · Milngin Ward supplementary by-election

NTEC’s 9 November notice confirms nominations are open and close at noon ACST on 20 November. Overseas postal vote applications close at 6 pm ACST on 29 November and local applications at 6 pm ACST on 1 December. The separate completed ballot receipt deadline remains noon ACST on 16 December.
