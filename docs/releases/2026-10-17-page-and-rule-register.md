## Keeping this reviewable

- Update a page entry only when that page or its implementation changes. Keep work-started and first-verified dates historical.
- Keep observation, publication, source-check, boundary-edition, rule-version and page-update dates distinct. Regeneration alone does not refresh a fact.
- Append what changed, why, sources and actual validation to the place history and refresh log. Preserve earlier entries.
- When a registered review input changes, repeat affected checks and record their evidence before updating the acceptance fingerprints.
- Review before publishing and when a listed trigger occurs; no automatic future review or monitoring is scheduled by this register.
