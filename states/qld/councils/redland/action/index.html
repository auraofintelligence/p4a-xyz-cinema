Follow the legal source trail

A shared pattern for making it real.

01

Notice and map

Record the place, time and lived experience. Protect home addresses, health information and other people's privacy. Similar reports are a lead to investigate, not automatically a mandate.

02

Compare possible changes

Use the civic twin as an inspectable scenario: show assumptions, alternatives, dependencies and uncertainty. Link the relevant legal source, service provider and decision pathway.

03

Agree the work

Identify who can authorise it, paid and voluntary roles, resources, learning, time and review points. A simulation or draft agreement is not permission to act.

04

Try, observe and learn

Record delivery separately from a measured result. Preserve disagreement and corrections. Hand over enough context that another human or agent can continue.

What it cost

Identify a budget, funding source, paid work, materials and maintenance. Do not put a zero where an estimate is missing.

What was given freely

Make care, local knowledge, learning and voluntary effort visible with consent. One verified human hour is counted once, even if it supports several goals.

What changed

Choose a baseline and a way to observe benefit, harm and time. Who gained access or agency? Who took on extra work?
