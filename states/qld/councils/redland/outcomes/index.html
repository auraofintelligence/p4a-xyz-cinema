What was given freely

Keep verified hours and other chosen contributions distinct. No fixed cash conversion or repeated claim for the same hour.

What changed

Look for access, care, ecology, confidence, ownership, relationships and time freed. Include burden shifted and those still excluded.

What exists in this example?

The journey, reviewed source maps, dated figures, searchable questions and connections, election clock and private-note download are implemented. There are no verified local project outcomes or issued C-Hours in this demonstration.

Local work can continue whether or not a by-election occurs, a person stands, or a proposed system is adopted. The record should explain what continued, changed or was handed over.

Repair stays visible.

A correction should keep the earlier claim, what changed, the evidence and the date. Kintsugi Protocol gives that practice a human meaning: repair can remain part of the story. A truthful record makes room for learning without hiding responsibility.
