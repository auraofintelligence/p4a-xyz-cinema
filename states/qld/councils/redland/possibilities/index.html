The songs in A Protopian Gambit connect personal reflection with care, place and public responsibility. Follow a title for its current lyrics and videos, or explore the full 22-song album.

Kintsugi Protocol finds a way forward through cracks, mistakes and repair. Choose Your Own Protopia invites people to shape their own future; Adaptable Yes gives consent room to open, pause and change. Don't Try to Fix Me insists on agency over the body, personal data and how a person is understood.

Joyful Responsible Abundance brings shared learning, transparency and care into the same picture. Hold the Light imagines memory and technology helping love and dignity endure. The Purple Mat that Could carries an ordinary comic frustration into self-reflection; Don't Throw a Brick turns anger towards public questions, records and accountable action.

Shifting Sands of Timeless Redlands keeps Country, water, history and belonging present. Every Border a Bridge carries music and learning between cultures. We Go Beyond (She Her) and We Go Beyond (He Him) gather those threads into a journey from personal reflection and local care towards wider civic stewardship.

A Conversations Review reflects on human-AI dialogue, error correction and learning together. 60 Days Set in Stone imagines sustained personal reflection and an Aura twin; the optional Aura Genesis journey remains within the people and candidates path.
