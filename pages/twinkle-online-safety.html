Explore the policy idea

Short video script

The platform can recognise three seconds of a song, but the bloke threatening a kid is somehow a complex case. A platform’s rules affect livelihoods and daily relationships. A person should be able to understand an action, find a responsible human and contest a mistake. Require understandable safety decisions, a working complaint route and a human appeal for significant mistakes.
