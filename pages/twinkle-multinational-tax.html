Short video script

A multinational can lose its profits somewhere offshore, but I'd better have a receipt for those work socks. Revenue, taxable profit and tax paid are different figures. Source-linked legal and ownership records could turn a multinational tax headline into a question people can actually examine. Make major corporate tax claims traceable and fund the expertise needed to examine them.
