Where you live, what you do and the people you care for lead to different parts of the law. Describe your circumstances, then take a clear research brief to an AI or a person helping you investigate.

Explore council, state or territory and Commonwealth requirements, and record where they create friction. This builds a research starting point, not an exhaustive finding about which laws bind you.

P4A aims to grow from a civic workbench into an active grassroots political party, with people connected across councils, wards, state and federal electorates. This is a starting point for prospective members and anyone exploring their civic life: understand your circumstances, research the rules and choose what you want to contribute.
