Short video script

We've agreed the marriage is over; the lawyers reckon it's still got a few good years in it. A shared-household agreement would make care, property, consent and exit understandable. Global Group Marriages explores voluntary adult household forms; it does not require everybody to live the same way. Make separation processes more understandable and affordable, and develop optional household agreements for people who want to share care and resources.
