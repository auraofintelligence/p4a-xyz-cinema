The serious room underneath

Australia’s Marriage Act defines marriage as a union of two people, exclusive of all others. Divorce under the Family Law Act is distinct from resolving parenting or property. No-fault divorce does not promise an automatic equal property split or an automatic parenting arrangement. Luke’s Australia atlas distinguishes present legal building blocks from a new multi-adult civil status.
