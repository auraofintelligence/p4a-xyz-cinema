Short video script

At the price of legal psychedelic therapy, I'd want to meet God and get him to chip in. The idea is a professionally governed treatment and research pathway with preparation and follow-up. The wider health and wellbeing projects provide questions and designs to examine, not guaranteed clinical results. Develop an independently evaluated care pilot within authorised pathways, with transparent costs, informed consent and follow-up.
