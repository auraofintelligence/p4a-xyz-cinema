Short video script

They said AI would give us more time with our families; now it rings pretending to be one of them. A convincing voice or video is not proof of identity. The connected idea is practical verification plus responsibility across the services that spread the scam and move the money. Make impersonation fraud a platform, telecom and payment-system responsibility as well as a literacy issue.
