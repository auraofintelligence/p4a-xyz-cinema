The plan is to let me raid my super for a house, so now I can be broke at two different ages. Taking money from super changes both the housing path and retirement path. A transparent model could compare the whole household outcome with supply, tenure and shared-ownership alternatives. Publish a transparent household comparison before supporting a super-for-housing scheme.
