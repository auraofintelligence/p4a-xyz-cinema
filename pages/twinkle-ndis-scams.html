The serious room underneath

NDIA distinguishes deliberate fraud from mistakes and non-compliance. Its public guidance covers misleading claims and misuse of funds; invoices, service agreements and dates can help investigators assess a concern. An undelivered or duplicated service claim needs checking, but a billing dispute or unfamiliar disability support is not automatically fraud.
