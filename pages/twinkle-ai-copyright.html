Short video script

My work is 'training data' when AI companies take it and 'intellectual property' when they sell it back. AI training, generated outputs and cloning a person’s identity are different uses. Personal permission profiles and source-linked legal tools offer ways to make those choices and disputes understandable. Make training and reuse permissions visible, give creators collective bargaining options and provide a practical route to contest misuse.
