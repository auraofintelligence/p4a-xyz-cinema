Aura Genesis / Process and learning

Aura Genesis Protocol

A proposed process of guided reflection, personal AI and practical learning. Participants build and review a record of their experiences, then explore how they want to live, learn and contribute.

Open Aura Genesis Protocol (new tab)Understand the process

What the process proposes

  1. Bring in lived experience

    Choose stories, photographs, relationships, values and questions to work with. The participant decides what enters their personal record.

  2. Reflect, question and revise

    Structured AI dialogue explores memory, assumptions, grief, motives and possible futures. The participant reviews interpretations and can correct them. The proposed Aura Twin is the evolving record of that work, with permissions attached.

  3. Turn reflection into practice

    The learning journey adds studio tasks, review and teaching practice across six subject areas. It connects personal understanding with making, useful work and civic stewardship.

The session is part of a wider journey

The source schedule proposes sixty sessions containing two qualifying hours at pressure each, for 120 qualifying hours. Everyday activities around the sessions and the sixty day teacher-development programme have their own clocks.

The physical setting ranges from an existing seated chamber to proposed mineral and crystalline environments. Screens or VR/XR, haptic interaction, optional sensors and receiver hardware support the experience design. Engineering and operating requirements belong in the supporting research.

The linked Aura Genesis Protocol explains the process, learning progression, personal information permissions and research questions. Its source library preserves the original Protocol, development and teacher-training documents.

How it connects to P4A

  • Clinical pathways: the consent, evidence and professional responsibility needed before a health application could be used. The applications have their own rooms: dementia and aged care, and PTSD care and claims.
  • Political what-if: the candidate-mirror satire and its questions about public accountability. That room holds the democratic and privacy safeguards.
  • Alien Necklace film: the creative branch, contributor roles and participatory worldbuilding. Further interface ideas include a film pitch gateway and an Aura Director for testing scenes.

Genesis remains a design and research proposal. The clinical room explains how claims would need to be tested; the political and film rooms explain their own premises.
