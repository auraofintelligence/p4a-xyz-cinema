Short video script

My landlord's house made more than I did, and it spent the year sitting on its arse. Mutual Futures connects business succession, shared productive ownership, learning, care and time. A community wealth fund would retain assets and returns; patient finance could support viable transitions without demanding a quick sell-off. Mutual Futures asks whether viable businesses could pass into willing worker and member ownership as owners retire.
