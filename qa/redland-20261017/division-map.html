    What the map describes

    The official Queensland pastel raster shows actual coastlines, islands and places with subdued grey roads. Detailed roads switch to the official topographic style. Actual division polygons are a transparent overlay, including administrative water areas. Both share the same EPSG:3857 coordinates, aligned using the service's returned image extent. No SVG, traced outlines or synthetic geography. The retained PNG works locally; zoom-specific detail loads from the official Queensland map service when online. The official 2024 electoral-division layer supplies all ten Redland City divisions. The 2019 review map is retained separately as a historical source.
