# Redlands local Twinkle correction

Editorial review: 17 October 2026, Australia/Brisbane. Scope: the 24 `type: twinkle` records in `content/local/redland-connections.json`. This is an editorial correction, not a new check of the linked external sources.

## Rules actually found

No applicable `AGENTS.md` was found in the repository or workspace ancestry. The authoritative existing format is published in `content/rooms/twinkle.html`, under `#format` (lines 67–73 at review), with its credibility rule at `#ledger` (lines 51–54).

- **One scene:** an ordinary straight-to-camera setting. The quiet version is appropriate where care, harm or grief makes a gag inappropriate.
- **One gripe:** “Name the actual gripe in the title.” The one-liner stays on that same gripe, makes sense alone, and begins from an ordinary complaint people already voice. A room or tool concept alone is not a Twinkle.
- **One breath:** the short gripe opens the door; policy options, evidence and participation belong underneath in the connected serious room.
- **Punch at machinery:** systems, forms, deals and doors; never people, service, culture or grief.
- **Checkable:** endorsement requires evidence and a reasoned ledger record. A seed is not automatically endorsed or verified.
- **Film your own:** the reusable format remains open for adaptation to a person's actual place and checked experience.

`README.md` lines 168 and 185 additionally preserve the approved national copy and prohibit adding “bloody” or “bloody hell” to new copy. The existing national 49 entries were not changed. The authored 3 October records in `content/twinkle-reviewed-copy.json` supply the established headline plus short gripe pattern; `tools/apply-reviewed-twinkle-copy.py` is a completed migration and was not rerun.

## What broke the pattern

The local records used open research questions as titles and multi-sentence explanations as their opening copy. Several began from a tool or civic concept—digital twins, evidence trails, recording contribution or mayoral powers—rather than an ordinary complaint. The generic issue disclosure hid the potential comic seed among guidance, systems, sources and accounting. There was no explicit distinction between a draft seed and a ledger-endorsed Twinkle.

The existing material was useful serious-room context; it was misplaced as the Twinkle itself.

## Changes to the canonical records

All 24 stable IDs remain. Each title now names one everyday gripe, and `summary` holds its short, self-contained line. Each earlier title is retained verbatim as `question`; each earlier summary is retained verbatim as `context`.

The draft copy ranges from 7 to 20 words per line. That count is a practical editorial check, not an invented numerical rule from the existing format. The care-at-home record (`r15`) uses the quiet presentation. The other drafts aim their humour at processes and infrastructure.

Every seed now carries `seedStatus`: draft local wording, not a resident quotation, verified complaint or endorsed Civic Ledger entry. The root record carries the exact editorial sources and review date. External source check dates remain 16 October; editorial changes are dated 17 October.

Preserved without change: the 19 non-Twinkle records; all IDs; every place, source, related connection, action, next route, evidence limitation, lyric meaning and cost/free/change trinity. The broader Joyful Responsible Abundance narrative and existing lyric meanings remain in their contextual records.
