# Victoria source-use and licence review register

Checked 2 October 2026. Personal/public non-commercial civic research and workbench; not a registered political party or commercial service. Source-use review log, not a legal determination. No permissions were requested and no publication was performed.

The existing [project licence](../../LICENCE.md) is unchanged. It does not replace third-party conditions. The [machine-readable register](vic-source-register.json) contains the same assessments and asset locations.

## Recheck when purpose changes

- Change to an actual political party or party campaign operation
- Commercial use, sponsorship, advertising or paid access
- Fundraising connected to these datasets
- New bulk redistribution, syndication or copying source artwork

A trigger means reassess the particular source; it does not mean every CC BY dataset becomes prohibited. Do not infer commercial or party use from the current P4A branding when the operator has stated the present research purpose.
