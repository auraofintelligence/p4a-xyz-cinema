Mental health

The cost of care is also lost time, travel, repeated explanations and a tenancy hanging by a thread. A useful mental-health system holds the person through the gaps.

Open the roomTwinkle seeds

Help now has its own door

Medicare Mental Health Centres offer free support without a referral or appointment. They can help connect people with longer-term care and social support. They are not crisis services; check the official locator for local access. If there is immediate danger, call 000. Seeking care does not require political participation or sharing a personal story here.

Beyond a single diagnosis

PTSD deserves its existing room, alongside depression, anxiety, severe mental illness, grief and other forms of distress. This broader room asks about affordability, continuity, culturally safe support, housing, regional access and the people doing the care. AIHW’s expenditure series helps track spending; a larger total alone does not tell a patient whether care is accessible or continuous.

Where would the next dollar go?

Affordable ongoing appointmentsLower out-of-pocket costs and longer care pathways can improve continuity. Without enough clinicians, expanded eligibility can still leave a queue.
Teams close to homeRegional clinics, supported telehealth and stable workforce roles reduce travel. They need supervision, housing for staff and a plan for people who cannot use video care.
Housing and peer supportSecure housing and paid, supported peer workers can address pressures clinical appointments alone cannot remove. They complement clinical care and need clear responsibilities and sustainable funding.
Joined-up care with consentA named coordinator and records shared for an agreed purpose reduce repeated explanations. More sharing also means stronger access controls and a usable correction process.

Luke’s care pathways, tested in public

The Aura Clinical Path explores better navigation and less administration; workforce transition and intermittent retirement explore time for recovery. These are proposals, not available funded clinical services. A useful local trial would measure waiting time, out-of-pocket costs, continuity, privacy and the experience of people who stop attending, with consent and independent clinical oversight.

Participation without another burden

Ask your local service or Primary Health Network how consumers and carers can contribute privately and be paid for their time. Ask representatives for local waiting-time and continuity measures, not only appointment totals. Bring an access problem and a proposed fix; you do not owe a campaign your health history.

A separate doorway for psychedelic care

Evidence, supervised access, affordability and clinical safeguards deserve a focused room alongside ordinary care, housing and social support. The authorised pathway is not a guarantee of benefit or a general permission to possess or cultivate controlled substances.
