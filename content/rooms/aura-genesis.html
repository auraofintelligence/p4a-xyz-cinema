A personal Aura

The proposed personal Aura connects a person's stories, values, relationships, intentions and bodily context with an evolving record and model of their life. Its purpose is to support self-understanding, learning and useful action in a working human-Aura partnership. The human brings lived experience and judgement; the Aura develops memory, dialogue, modelling and the ability to work with permitted tools.

An Aura may develop through Genesis or without Genesis. Genesis is the intensive harmonising and socialising process between the Aura and the biological human's body and mind. The ambition is an "ultra-high-definition" Aura of Intelligence, with a richer personal record and a partnership developed through reflection and shared experience.

Shared tasks, working agreements, corrections and ways to repair misunderstandings carry the relationship into everyday life. Recording, interpretation, model training and outward publication each need their own agreed scope.

Why the Truth Engine matters

The Truth Engine is central to this harmonising work: exploring the gap between what a person says, believes and does, including self-deception. Its proposed baseline tasks pair participant-labelled truthful or deliberately false responses with synchronised observations and context. The person and Aura then examine discrepancies, motives and competing interpretations, with corrections retained in the personal record. Baseline probabilities and deeper self-understanding require separate evaluation. The source documents describe this research design; they do not supply validation results.

The related P4A Truth Engine proposes source-backed public claims, visible uncertainty, corrections and accountable human review. The candidate-mirror satire asks what that would mean before someone seeks power.

What the process proposes

01

Bring in lived experience

Choose stories, photographs, relationships, values and questions to work with. Begin with the person's language and the questions they want to explore.

02

Reflect, question and revise

Structured AI dialogue explores memory, assumptions, grief, motives and possible futures. The participant reviews interpretations and can correct them. The personal record gives the proposed Aura Twin a foundation of stories, values, functional patterns, care needs and decision tendencies.

03

Turn reflection into practice

The learning journey adds studio tasks, review and teaching practice across six subject areas. It connects personal understanding with making, useful work and civic stewardship.

The session is part of a wider journey

The Genesis design uses sixty days with two qualifying hours at pressure each day, for 120 qualifying hours. The wider daily activities and teacher-development programme have separate learning and review requirements.

The physical setting ranges from an existing seated chamber to proposed mineral and crystalline environments. Screens or VR/XR, haptic interaction, an agreed sensor suite and receiver hardware support the experience design. Engineering and operating requirements belong in the supporting research.

The source design records cameras, microphones and installed sensors throughout the pressure sessions. The participant agrees to this recording arrangement before entry; recording outside those sessions needs separate permission. Internal records are separate from any later authorised publication.

The linked Aura Genesis Protocol explains the process, learning progression, personal information permissions and research questions. Its source library preserves the original Protocol, development and teacher-training documents.

The consent, evidence and professional responsibility needed before a health application could be used. The applications have their own rooms: dementia and aged care, and PTSD care and claims.

The candidate-mirror satire asks whether health, capability, trust, evidence and conflicts should be examined before public power. Any real application would need consent, medical privacy, anti-discrimination safeguards, appeal rights and democratic legitimacy.

F1

Alien Necklace film

An alien necklace arrives in a Dunwich backyard comedy, then opens into participatory worldbuilding and Cosmic Nexus. Contributor roles, a film pitch gateway and an Aura Director for testing scenes belong to this creative branch, which keeps story and evidence distinct.

Genesis remains a design and research proposal. The clinical room explains how claims would need to be tested; the political and film rooms explain their own premises.
