Short video script

A woman gets told to leave a violent man, then asked where she plans to live. The proposed technology support belongs inside a human safety pathway. Recording, alerts and device checks would serve the person’s chosen exit plan, with one contact joining immediate help to a safe destination. An opt-in safety pathway could join AI-assisted smart-device recording and alerting, specialist phone checks for stalking software, and one-stop support for immediate exit.
