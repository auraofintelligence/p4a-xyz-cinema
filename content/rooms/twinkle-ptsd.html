Short video script

He came home with PTSD and got sent back through the worst of it, one claim form at a time. Care and claims administration should not make a person recount the same trauma to every office. One accountable human and an authorised record could keep the pathway together. Give each claimant one accountable human and start an appropriate care pathway while administration is resolved.
