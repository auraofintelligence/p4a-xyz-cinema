Short video script

Beer brought in more than petroleum resource rent tax. Apparently my schooner digs deeper than the gas giants. The joke is about who carries the tax burden and who receives Australia’s resource wealth. Beer receipts and PRRT are different taxes, but their same-year totals make the public-return question hard to ignore. The gripe is the imbalance: Australians’ beer attracted more excise and customs receipts than petroleum resource rent tax brought in.
