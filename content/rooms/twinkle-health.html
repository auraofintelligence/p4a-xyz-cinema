Short video script

The GP said I need less stress, so I asked if they bulk-bill. Ethical overcompliance means using useful checks to do more than the bare minimum. The idea includes prescribing checks, unusual-case investigation, consent and follow-up, with AI saving time and clinicians retaining judgement. A proposed standard of care would expect clinicians to use suitable, validated AI assistance when it improves ethical overcompliance: more thorough checks of consent, prescribing, interactions, follow-up and unusual problems.
