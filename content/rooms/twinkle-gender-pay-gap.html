Short video script

Women earn less than men on average, but somehow the landlord’s never heard of a gender rent gap. 500 Queens is a proposed women’s leadership and enterprise pathway, with mentoring, practical projects and a substantial source library. It adds ownership and decision-making to the question of pay and caring penalties. Make pay, promotion and caring penalties visible in public-sector and participating employer systems.
