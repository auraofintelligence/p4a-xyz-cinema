The serious room underneath

The Productivity Commission’s July 2026 dashboard marks the preschool-enrolment target as met. It also reports 33.9% of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander children commencing school developmentally on track in 2024, and 81.4% of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people living in appropriately sized housing in 2021. These are different populations, measures and years; progress in one does not settle the others. The Coalition of Peaks places shared decision-making and stronger community control at the centre of reform.
