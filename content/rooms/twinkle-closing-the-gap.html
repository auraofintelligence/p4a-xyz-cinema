We've got enough reports on Indigenous disadvantage to build a bridge across the gap. Community control means authority over decisions and resources, not merely another consultation. Sovereign data tools could help communities retain control while sharing information they choose. Place agreed services and resources under community-controlled authority, with long-term funding and locally defined outcomes.
