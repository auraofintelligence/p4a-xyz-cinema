The serious room underneath

UNFPA’s 2025 survey across 14 countries identifies financial constraints, housing, job insecurity, relationships and fears about the future among barriers to desired family size. Those international results are not an Australian prevalence estimate. ABS birth and fertility statistics measure births, not the number of children people wished to have; intentions need their own evidence.
