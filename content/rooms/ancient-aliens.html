Ancient aliens and UAP: fair inquiry, no appointed oracle

Keep the developed disclosure, civilian investigation, Sky Country and Empty Chair proposals together under an evidence-first remit. Belief, interpretation and verified observation remain distinct.

The Empty Chair is a discussion method for interests or perspectives we may have overlooked. It gives nobody authority to speak for an unknown being, and keeps inquiry separate from a presumed explanation.

How it could work

  1. Use named original records and willing witnesses to develop bounded investigation tasks and traceable public responses.
  2. Fund voluntary Indigenous-led research pilots with knowledge permissions and community control.
  3. Apply the Empty Chair as a recorded decision exercise: whose interests might be missing and which irreversible harms can be avoided?

Everyday example

An archive question receives a sourced reply; a research group can compare explanations without granting any person authority to speak for an unknown being.

Who could act and how it could be funded

National disclosure needs Commonwealth action; states, archives and institutions can participate in agreed research. Publish separate budgets for records, civilian inquiry and locally controlled pilots.

Fair-go safeguards

No conclusion of non-human origin is presumed. No unknown entity receives a vote or veto. Religious freedom and freedom to question coexist; cultural permissions remain local.

What would show progress

Records traceable; questions resolved; permissions honoured; safeguards changed by the exercise; costs and unresolved evidence.

Australia should take the lead.

P4A proposes a Minister for UAP/NHI Disclosure and Investigation, responsible for Australian records, independent civilian inquiry and public accountability. UAP means unidentified anomalous phenomena; NHI means non-human intelligence.

Open the records.

Locate reports, release what can lawfully be released and explain missing or withheld evidence. Publish the programme's costs, findings and unanswered questions for parliamentary scrutiny.

Test: can the public trace a named encounter from report to government response?

Investigate independently.

Fund civilian examination of encounters across land, air and sea, combining willing witnesses, original records and scientific testing.

Whistleblower reform: confidential reporting, legal assistance, retaliation remedies and statutory immunity for qualifying good-faith disclosures through an authorised process. Add an independently reviewable safeguard against extradition used to punish those disclosures. These are proposed reforms, excluding unrelated serious wrongdoing; Australian protection cannot bind foreign courts.

Current law and sources

The PID Act protects eligible disclosures through specified routes, not unrestricted publication or a discloser's own wrongdoing. Intelligence information has additional restrictions; contact IGIS before supplying classified material. Extradition depends on the Act and applicable treaties. Section 7 objections are not a general whistleblower exemption.

The choice: use existing agencies, create a dedicated civilian office, or fund independent research. Compare cost, access to classified evidence and independence before setting the portfolio's budget.

Sky Country and deep Country.

Fund a voluntary, Indigenous-led national partnership to catalogue and compare Sky Country and underground or underworld Dreaming accounts that knowledge holders choose to share. Begin with paid local pilots, then review whether to expand.

  • Community control: Elders and Indigenous organisations set questions, hold collections and decide access, reuse and withdrawal.
  • Keep context: approved attribution, Country, language, original words, reviewed translations and story variants accompany oral histories, song, dance and art.
  • Purpose: compare encounters, journeys, landscape changes and survival knowledge to develop agreed research questions and preparedness exercises.

National access must follow local permissions. Restricted knowledge stays with its custodians.

The Empty Chair.

A proposed philosophy and discussion protocol for empathy, black swan preparedness, and existential threat avoidance. Leave a figurative seat for unknown non-human perspectives. Work through decisions as if observation or interference exists, without assigning an identity, intention, vote or veto.

Why give it a place? It is a low-cost way to question human-centred assumptions while identity and origin remain unknown. Potential benefits are wider empathy, earlier recognition of overlooked interests and more reversible decisions.

Pitfalls: speculation could crowd out evidence, self-appointed spokespeople could claim authority, or the chair could become empty symbolism. Nobody speaks or vetoes on behalf of an unknown being.

Try it on a decision: before approving an investigation or disturbing a site, ask whose interests are absent, which assumption could fail and what irreversible harm to avoid. Record whether the exercise changed a safeguard or research question. Judge its usefulness from that result.

Possible origins remain open: extra-terrestrial, crypto-terrestrial (hidden on or within Earth), or interdimensional. A portal into a cave or temple could mark an arrival point rather than a place of origin. What evidence would distinguish these possibilities?

Australian remit, regional cooperation.

Include the mainland and external territories, including Christmas Island, Cocos (Keeling), Norfolk and the Coral Sea Islands. Across Asia, Oceania and the Indian Ocean, share research with willing partners who retain authority over their records.

Territories and Antarctica

Australian Antarctic work must follow the Antarctic Treaty's peaceful scientific framework and its preservation of differing sovereignty positions. Australian administration does not imply universal recognition of its claim.

Gods, angels or visitors?

Call it a flying saucer and people laugh; call it a fiery chariot and it gets a stained-glass window. What if some, or even all, ancient gods were encounters with non-human intelligence, and angels were visitors or intermediaries?

Faith matters, but it is not an excuse to switch your brain off and discount rational inquiry.

Ezekiel's wheels and eyes: chapters 1 and 10 describe a vision, wheels within wheels, eyes, a throne and cherubim. Compare divine encounter, symbolic throne and engineered/ET readings. Which details fit each interpretation, and what independent evidence would change it?

Verses and competing readings

Ezekiel 1:15-21 describes wheels and eyes; 1:26-28 places a figure above them. Chapter 10:9-20 returns to the wheels and identifies cherubim. Christine Hayes explains the mobile throne through exile and ancient Near Eastern imagery. Sensors, machinery and occupants are proposed modern correspondences.

Who gets authority?

A spectacular arrival does not settle who should govern. Who interprets the message, who benefits, and are people free to refuse? Protect freedom to believe, question and leave; investigate coercion and exploitation. The same questions apply to AI gods and superintelligence.

Belief, coercion and public accountability

Jonestown makes coercive authority a concrete public concern. The FBI records abuse allegations, requests to leave and the killing of Leo Ryan. The Human Rights Commission explains freedom of belief and freedom from coercion. Ask how public funding, complaints and independent support are handled.
