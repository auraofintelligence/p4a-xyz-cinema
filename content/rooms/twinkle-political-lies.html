They can fit “authorised by” into every election ad; I’d love to see them have a crack at “true”. The Truth Engine idea connects a checkable claim to evidence, uncertainty and correction. Independent review would need to distinguish a factual assertion from a forecast, opinion or joke. Create a public claim ledger and propose independent, timely adjudication of checkable campaign claims.
