Dementia and aged care

Digital memoirs, everyday preferences and support for the person and their carers. This room explores a consent-led research pathway, with evidence required before any claim of benefit.

Whose story, whose choices?

The person receiving care stays at the centre. Record what they want others to know, support communication and involve chosen carers with an appropriate consent or lawful decision-making process. A useful trial would ask whether the records help everyday care and whether people can correct, export or stop using them.

Real de-slop

Start with the person and their care.

The proposal begins with a life story the person chooses to record. A research pathway would need a clinical wrapper: structured evidence, consent, safety, privacy, clinician-readable reports and clear limits on what the system can claim.

Three practical pathways

MemoryDigital memoirs

The proposed memory map would bring together chosen photos, stories, places, songs, values, routines and family knowledge, with clear consent settings.

CareDementia support

A research trial could assess quality of life, daily function, distress, chosen prompts and carer burden over time. Benefits would need to be demonstrated.

WellbeingHealth readiness

Explore optional reflection and sensor summaries for discussions about sleep, stress, social connection and support needs with a qualified care team.

Australian health stack

Medicare and PBS

Stay legible to the existing system: GP conversations, MBS-style reports where appropriate, medicine context through PBS-aware records, and no pretending every cost can be wished away.

Private insurance pressure

Private premiums rose by an approved industry average of 4.41% from 1 April 2026. Aura should argue for prevention, transparency and value, not another mystery add-on fee.

NDIS and younger-onset dementia

For eligible people under 65, support pathways may involve NDIS, assistive technology evidence, goals, function, safety and clear professional justification.

My Aged Care and carers

For older Australians, the value case must include carer wellbeing, respite logic, practical daily function and reports that help families navigate care.

Clinical evidence ladder

Phase 1Feasibility with small participant-carer dyads: usability, consent, safety, comprehension and whether people actually use it.
Phase 2Pilot study with clinical partners: quality-of-life, function, carer burden and report usefulness.
Phase 3Regulatory and funding evidence: technical file, privacy, cybersecurity, human factors and professional adoption.

Anti-slop rules

Do not diagnose from vibes.
Do not call a mirror a doctor.
Do not sell surveillance as care.
Do not make families repeat the same story to every system.

Source anchors

Medicare is the universal health insurance scheme, PBS medicines sit inside Medicare access, private insurance premiums changed in 2026, and NDIS funds eligible disability supports. Medical software claims would need the TGA software-based medical device pathway and human research needs ethics discipline.
