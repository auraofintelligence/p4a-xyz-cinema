Short video script

When I hire a trailer, they explain the risks; spend billions on national security and suddenly I'm too unqualified to ask. Security includes whether ordinary systems keep working and whether people can repair or replace them. Distributed skills, local capability and diverse dependencies offer a wider set of questions than a purchase price. Assess security as a portfolio of threats, dependencies and useful resilience, with explicit failure cases and review triggers.
