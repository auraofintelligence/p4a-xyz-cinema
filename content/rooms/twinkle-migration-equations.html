We've got a migration target, a housing target and a transport plan; be nice if someone introduced them. People add demand and capability together. A living model of homes, skills, services and delivery times could help population decisions and investment stop talking past one another. Use joined-up local capacity scenarios to inform national population decisions and state investment.
