I'm trying to watch the footy, but the gambling ads keep teaching me how to lose the grocery money responsibly. The gripe is gambling promotion taking over the game and its aftermath. Product rules, advertising, community revenue and useful support belong in the same discussion. Reduce inducements and exposure, make limits work across participating products and fund help independently of gambling promotion.
