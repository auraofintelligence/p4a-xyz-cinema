Short video script

I've paid so much in tolls the tag should open the boardroom. A toll-road contract decides much more than the next price rise. A public register and AI-assisted reading could make the deal understandable enough for people to question it. Put each toll-road deal on a readable public register, including public contributions, price rises, ownership, expiry and review rights.
