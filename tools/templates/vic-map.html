State Parliament and local councils: different responsibilities.

Legislative Assembly: 88 districts elect one MP each. Legislative Council: eight regions elect five MLCs each; each region contains 11 Assembly districts. The two state layers describe Parliament. The local-council and ward layers describe municipal government.

Local-government councils are separate municipalities. Choose Local councils for 79 municipalities and eight separately labelled unincorporated areas.       What does a majority mean?

      45 of 88 Assembly seats and 21 of 40 Council seats are majorities of all seats. These markers do not say how many votes every motion needs. Ordinary decisions depend on the majority of members voting; attendance, abstentions and special rules can affect the outcome.

      In the Assembly, the Speaker votes only to break a tie. In the Council, the President has an ordinary vote and a tied motion fails. A party's current seat count is not a guarantee of how its members will vote. Government depends on the confidence of the Assembly; a minority government can hold that confidence.
